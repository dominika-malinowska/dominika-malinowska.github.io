My paper describing the first part of my PhD reserach is now published! 

---

In the paper, we introduce a method for predicting the availability of measurement points provided by Synthetic Aperture Radar. 

By leveraging interferometric coherence decay characteristics and using a U-Net convolutional neural network architecture, our approach tackles the challenge of non-uniform distribution of measurement points across buildings and infrastructure networks. This can support better decision-making by offering stakeholders a tool to assess the feasibility of applying InSAR monitoring to specific structures.
