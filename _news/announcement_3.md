A proud moment in my PhD journey! The Nature Communications paper I led has been selected for the Editors' Highlights in the "Engineering and Infrastructure" collection 🚀 

---

This collection aims to highlight a small number of the best papers recently published that the editors consider particularly interesting or important.

Congratulations again to my co-authors and supervisory team: Pietro Milillo, Cormac Reale, Chris Blenkinsopp, and Giorgia Giardina.

I'm honoured to be featured alongside such high-impact work, and I highly recommend browsing the full collection to explore the other studies.
